Overview

How a sale flows through the system

A cashier scans an item on the POS Terminal. The request passes through the JWT Auth Gateway, lands on the Sales Service, and is committed inside one atomic transaction: sale created, stock deducted, ledger entry written. Only after that commit does Sales Service publish a SaleCompletedEvent — everything downstream of that (loyalty points, analytics, low-stock checks) happens independently, without slowing down the cashier. Click any block above to read what it does.

What sets this build apart
Event-driven checkout

Checkout publishes one event instead of calling five services — new side effects never touch the checkout path.

Concurrency-safe stock

Optimistic locking + WebSocket broadcast stop two terminals from overselling the same last unit.

Immutable ledger

Stock is never just overwritten — every change is a logged, auditable movement record.

Offline-first terminal

A dropped connection never blocks a sale; queued sales sync with idempotency keys on reconnect.
